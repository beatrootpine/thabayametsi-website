The reserve lies in the Waterberg’s bird-rich sourveld, and the kloof, with its deep soil and tall trees, holds the forest species.

This is the start of the reserve’s bird checklist. The field guide keeps it and updates it each season. The downloadable list will be published once it is complete.

## In the kloof

Crested barbet, often heard before it is seen. Grey-headed bushshrike. Narina trogon, on occasion. The owls, after dark.

## On the cliffs and over the ridge

Verreaux’s eagle nests on the cliffs of the district and hunts over the ridge, where the dassies are on every warm rock.
