Spring on the reserve runs from September to October, and it is the season of waiting. The grass on the lower slopes is the colour of the winter that has just ended. The ridge is dry. Then, on the warm rock, the first flowers come out before any rain has fallen.

The kudu bulls are in their best condition of the year. We see them on the open ground below the kloof at first light, necks thick, moving between the cows that keep to the kloof edge.

## What the first storm does

The first storm usually comes in the afternoon, out of the north-west. When it does, the change on the reserve is quick. The springs below the ridge run again within days, the grass comes up green under the burnt blocks first, and the steenbok and duiker move out onto it.
